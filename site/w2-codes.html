What's that code on your W-2?

Box 12 of your W-2 is a row of two-letter codes with dollar amounts. Type one in and get the plain-English meaning — including the three brand-new codes that start appearing on W-2s in January 2027.

New in 2026: code TP (your reported tips), code TT (your qualified overtime premium), and code TA (employer money into a child's Trump account). TP and TT feed the new tips and overtime deductions — see what they're worth to you. If you earn tips or overtime and these boxes are missing or look wrong in January, raise it with payroll: for 2026, what's in these boxes is what you can claim.
